how it works

Hold the art.
Earn the market.

You buy a unique digital art NFT. That piece becomes your key to a pool that earns tokenized stocks, and the pool is designed to follow the real art market.

Buy the piece

A one-of-a-kind digital artwork on Robinhood Chain. Owning it is what lets you earn.

Earn tokenized stocks

The pool tied to your piece is designed to route tokenized AAPL, NVDA, TSLA, and GME to whoever holds it.

Tied to the art market

When auction results and art indices climb, the pool routes in more. When the market cools, earnings slow. Sell the piece and the earning rights go with it.
